Quick-Start Analytical Workflow

  1. 1. Import Data: Upload `.txt`, `.xy`, `.csv`, `.tsv`, `.mzML`, or `.mzXML` spectra, or load the built-in synthetic native N-glycan validation spectrum.
  2. 2. Configure Library & Calibration: Select or generate a biosynthetically constrained human N-glycan library and optionally perform internal/external polynomial mass calibration.
  3. 3. MS1 Glycan or Glycopeptide Search: Run Mode A (Targeted) or Mode B (Library) search with transparent ppm/Da tolerance, multi-adduct expansion, and explicit ambiguity reporting.
  4. 4. Quantify, QC & Export: Inspect base-peak and relative abundances, MassyTools-inspired QC metrics, multivariate statistics, and download the 12-category export bundle.

Scientific Integrity & Scope Safeguards

  • Composition vs. Structure: MS1 mass matches identify monosaccharide compositions (`Hex`, `HexNAc`, `dHex`, `Neu5Ac`, `Neu5Gc`), not full glycosidic linkage or branching topologies unless supported by linkage-specific derivatization or MS/MS.
  • Proton vs. Neutral Hydrogen: Proton mass ($1.0072764522\text{ Da}$) and neutral $\text{H}$ atom mass ($1.0078250322\text{ Da}$) are strictly distinguished across all charge and adduct calculations.
  • Released vs. Peptide-Attached Glycans: Intact glycopeptide calculations strictly subtract the reducing-end $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ ($18.010565\text{ Da}$) present on free released glycans.
  • Zero Silent Fabrication: Peak areas and profile S/N are never fabricated on centroid-only peak lists, and spectra are never silently calibrated.

Peptide-Attached Glycan Moiety Convention

            

              Attached Moiety Rule: Unlike free released N-glycans (which contain a reducing-end $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ of $18.010565\text{ Da}$), N-glycans covalently attached to asparagine in a glycopeptide omit the terminal $\text{H}_2\text{O}$:
              $$\text{Mass}_{\text{glycopeptide}} = \text{Mass}_{\text{peptide}} + \sum \text{Mass}_{\text{monosaccharide residues}}$$
